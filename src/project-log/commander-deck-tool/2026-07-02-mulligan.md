title: "Mulligan heuristics"
date: 2026-07-02
tags: [simulation]

Simulation now models the London mulligan instead of always keeping the first seven. Needed a rule for what counts as a keepable hand.

Current heuristic is crude — two to five lands and at least one castable spell by turn three. Real players keep and mulligan for reasons this doesn't capture, but it's closer than always-keep.
