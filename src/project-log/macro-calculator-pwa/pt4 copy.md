Once I finshed the entry screens, it was onto simply displaying the data! I did this using a .where clause for Dexie, and the "where" being the current date. It would then look over the entries table and search for those entries that had the current date. Using the arrows, you can adjust it to go back a few days or fast forward. 

Part of the specifications of the app was that the day view didn't show the food I ate. It is less about the actual, specific food and more about the macro-nutrients I am taking in.
