Defined the first version: users can create habits, check them off once per day, and see a simple streak count. No accounts, no sync — everything stays on device.

The main constraint is scope. If I try to ship reminders, social features, and cloud backup in v1, I'll never finish. Keeping it local-only also means I can skip auth entirely.
